How the model prices each row

implied = TVL × peerMultiple × growthMult × maturityMult × chainMult

  • peerMultiple — the median mcap/TVL of live-token peers in the same category, shrunk toward the global median
  • growthMult — this week's TVL change, clamped; a fall is penalised harder than a rise is rewarded
  • maturityMult — how long the protocol has been live; newer protocols read lower
  • chainMult — 1 plus 0.04 per extra chain, capped
